Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

Who we are

Lubnaig LLC is a Wyoming limited liability company. Our registered address is 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801. You can contact us at hello@lubnaig.com.

Lubnaig provides operational consulting to businesses (AI enablement: audit, pilot and retainer). Our work is performed from the United Kingdom as well as for clients in the United States and elsewhere. This notice explains how we handle personal data.

The data we handle

We typically handle business contact data, such as your name, job title, firm, work email, work phone, postal address, and messages you send us. If you become a client, we also handle billing details, contract records, and the business information you give us for an engagement.

We do not need client personal data, or your customers' personal data, to run an AI Capacity Audit. We work from volumes, timings and process maps. Sample documents should be redacted, or reviewed live on screen. If a later pilot or retainer may touch personal data, we will say so in writing first, sign any required data processing terms, and work inside your systems where practicable.

We do not buy marketing lists. We obtain names, roles, company details, and work contact information from public business registries and publicly available information about organizations and their business activities. We verify work email addresses before use.

Why we use personal data

Lubnaig LLC is the controller for personal data we use to run our business. We use business contact details to respond to inquiries, manage relationships with client organizations, deliver services, and administer billing. For contacts acting for an organization, we rely on our legitimate interests in running and administering our consulting business. If you personally enter into a contract with us or ask us to take steps before entering into one, we use your data as necessary for those purposes. We keep records where the law requires us to do so and use contact and technical information as necessary for our legitimate interests in operating and securing our website and email.

Outreach and choices

Where permitted, we use business contact information to send relevant business marketing based on our legitimate interest in finding clients. We obtain consent where required. A work email address does not, on its own, mean we have permission to email you. You may object to direct marketing or withdraw consent at any time using the opt-out in the message or by emailing hello@lubnaig.com. We stop direct marketing to you when you object or withdraw consent and keep the minimum information needed to honor your choice. Every commercial email includes our postal address and a simple way to opt out. We honor opt-outs promptly, and in any event within 10 business days.

Where it is processed

Data may be processed in the United States and in the United Kingdom, and by carefully chosen processors who host email, documents or our website. Where UK GDPR requires a transfer safeguard for data leaving the UK, we use an appropriate mechanism, such as an approved standard contractual clause. You may ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguard by emailing hello@lubnaig.com, subject to any necessary redactions.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records while an inquiry is active and for 24 months after the last substantive contact. We review prospect records at least every 12 months and delete them when they are no longer needed for outreach or when you object, subject to a suppression record so we do not contact you again. We keep client, contract and billing records for 7 years from the end of the tax year in which the engagement ends, or longer if the law requires. Website logs and security records are kept for up to 12 months unless a longer period is needed to investigate an incident.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with processors who help us run the business (for example email, hosting and accounting tools), with professional advisors, or if the law requires it. We use only AI services that contractually bar training on client data, and we name those services in writing before a pilot that may touch your information.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, restrict its use, receive it in a portable format, or object to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time where we rely on it. Withdrawal takes effect for the future and does not affect use already made lawfully. In the United Kingdom the regulator is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). To use these rights, email hello@lubnaig.com.

Website technology

Our website is a static site. We do not currently use analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or email open or click tracking. If that changes, we will update this notice and obtain consent where required before activating those tools.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change and will change the date above. The updated notice applies from the date it is published. It is not a negotiated contract term.
