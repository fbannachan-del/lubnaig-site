Fair questions

What do you need from us?

A sponsor, four or five process owners for 45-minute interviews, and about six hours of your team's time in total.

Will this reduce headcount?

Their capacity, their choice. You can use the freed hours to grow without hiring, cut overtime and contractor spend, or run a leaner team over time through natural attrition. Every option is legitimate, and we will be straight with you about the numbers.

Where do the savings come from?

Mostly from time: fewer hours on repeatable work means less overtime, fewer contractor and agency hours, and room to grow without adding roles. Then fewer handoffs, less rework and faster turnaround. The audit shows each one in your numbers.

How quickly will we see results?

The audit takes two weeks. A Pilot Sprint puts the first process live in five weeks after that, and we measure it 30 days after go-live.

What if the audit finds less than 20% recoverable time?

You pay no audit fee. If you have already paid any of the fee, we refund everything you paid. The report is yours.

Do you sell software?

No. We charge fixed fees for the work and take no vendor commissions.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. The audit needs no client personal data: we work from volumes, timings and process maps, and you redact any sample documents. If a pilot touches client data, we work inside your systems and name each AI service and its data terms in writing first. We use only services that bar training on your data, and we tell you about any incident without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours.

What if we are not ready for AI?

That is fine. Start with a 30-minute call, and we will tell you honestly what is worth doing first.
