This is Loch Lubnaig.

A real loch in the Trossachs, just north of Callander. You are looking at its true shape, drawn from open elevation data, in the light and weather it has right now.

Fair questions

Will this reduce headcount?

You choose. Some clients use the freed hours to grow without hiring. Others reduce headcount and workforce overhead, through natural attrition, fewer contractors or a smaller team. Both are legitimate, and we will be straight with you about the numbers either way.

Where do the savings come from?

The biggest one is people cost: fewer hours needed means fewer roles, less overtime and lower agency and contractor spend. Then fewer handoffs, less rework, faster turnaround and less spend on outsourced or overflow work. We show you each one in the assessment, with numbers.

How quickly will we see results?

Most clients see a first workflow live within a few weeks of the assessment, then we expand from there.

What if we are not ready for AI?

That is fine. The assessment tells you where you stand and what is worth doing first. No obligation.
